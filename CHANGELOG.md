# Changelog

All notable changes to Context Atlas are documented here. The project follows Semantic Versioning.



## v0.2.0 - 2026-09-06
### Added
* Country portraits compare exact-year indicator values and published trends for two countries, with source details, location maps, and reproducible share links.
* World Bank population and prosperity imports preserve original JSON, producer attribution, country-year notes and exact values across portraits, charts, exports and immutable refreshes.
* Map playback advances through exact calendar years with Play and Pause, preloads the next frame, preserves missing years and stops at the end or when the selection changes.
* Public MIT source and documented container releases, with CI checks before publishing and an operator-run Dokploy deployment script that verifies the exact version and HTTPS health without sharing the deployment credential with GitHub.


## v0.1.0 - 2026-08-27

### Added

- Initial Context Atlas public website, WHO data pipeline, explorer, maps, exact-year associations,
  exports, owner administration, and release-ready operational foundation.
